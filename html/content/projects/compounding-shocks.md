# Compounding climate shocks in Bangladesh

Does a drought early in life followed by a flood or cyclone effect child growth and survival more than either shock alone, and does the order matter?

Children in Bangladesh often face a slow-onset shock such as drought or humid heat followed by a sudden one such as a flood or cyclone. The effects of single hazards are well documented, but sequences are not. I test whether compound exposure between late pregnancy and age five is worse than the sum of its parts, and whether drought followed by flood is more harmful than the reverse. Child records from the 2017–18 and 2022 Bangladesh Demographic and Health Surveys are linked month by month to cyclone tracks, satellite flood maps, drought indices and heat-stress measures. Outcomes are wasting and under-five mortality.

- **Data** BDHS 2017–18, 2022
- **Hazards** Drought, flood, cyclone, heat
