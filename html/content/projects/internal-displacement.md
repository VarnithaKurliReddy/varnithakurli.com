# Mapping Internal Displacement From Environmental Disasters

A global map of floods, storms, wildfires and landslides, and the share of exposed people each event displaced or deceased.

## Abstract

Projections of climate migration rest on thin evidence about how many exposed people actually move. I map the recorded footprint of each displacement event from 2014 to 2023 and estimate the ratio of people displaced to people exposed, separating exposure from displacement itself.

Quantile models of the upper tail give expected displacement and mortality rates for the most severe events by disaster type, a baseline that humanitarian planners and funders can use. I also test whether conflict and subnational human development shift these rates.

- **Coverage** Global, 2014–2023
- **Events** 3,512
